## Intent and scope

Separate guild participation from operating or personally staking into a guild's
reactor. Guilds collect rank-sale proceeds and manage collective assets and energy
infrastructure. Players can participate without managing reactor selection.
Owner diversity rewards spreading usable reactor power; a stake-share cap limits
additional concentration once its activation threshold is reached.

Included:

- Guild and player vaults.
- Guild ownership and management of providers, agreements, substations and allocations.
- Configurable rank purchases using the guild's own token.
- A distinct, optional admission route through an initial rank purchase.
- One paid guild-creation path replacing proof-of-work and reactor entitlements.
- Flat 1:1 reactor base power, current owner diversity bonuses, and a sticky
  stake-share cap. No historical deposit rates or efficiency entitlements.
- Existing-state migration, updated public interfaces, security review, and all
  affected tests, including detailed live-chain and upgrade-script review.

Excluded:

- Defining what benefits or social roles guild ranks represent.
- Additional on-chain block-by-block billing history, historical verification,
  financial-history snapshots and retention scheduling.
- Changes to the existing time-based agreement/provider payment model.
- A new rank-benefits system or automatic substation reassignment on purchase.
- Substation creation costs, development upgrades, upkeep, decay and sabotage
  economics. Fixed 10% operating loss and guild ownership/management of
  substations remain included.
- Rank subscriptions, expiry, automatic renewal or time-based billing.

Frontend implementation and leaderboard implementation have not been separately
scoped. The chain must expose the owner, vault, pricing, stake-share and diversity information
needed by clients; cross-repository work requires identifying those repositories.

## Agreed gameplay and economic behavior

### Vaults and ownership

- Give both players and guilds protocol-controlled vaults for assets. Signing
  addresses remain identities through which players exercise permissioned control.
- New player asset activity should ordinarily use the player vault, including
  earned Alpha, reactor infusions and generator fuel purchases. Ore remains
  managed through the grid. The existing player-inventory Alpha field reports
  spendable vault Alpha; signing-key balances and stake remain separately queryable.
- Guilds can infuse, transfer assets, mint their token and manage the listed
  energy infrastructure on their own behalf.
- Guild vaults start empty during migration: do not appropriate a guild owner's
  personal assets or treat existing token collateral as treasury funds.
- Treasury funds, guild-token collateral, provider earnings, consumer collateral
  and staking pools remain separate accounts and obligations.
- Administrative guild-token minting goes to the guild vault. The mint message has
  no recipient override; distribution is a separate authorized vault transfer.

### Rank pricing and purchase

Agreed policy:

- Payment uses only the target guild's own token and goes to that guild's vault.
- Purchases are permanent unless the player is demoted; there is no timed rank.
- Guilds configure a formula through a small number of parameters, rather than
  maintaining a mandatory price list for every rank.
- Preserve the proposal's configurable purchase ceiling. Guilds define what
  ranks mean through the existing systems; do not introduce a new benefits model.

Implementation choice under the configurable-formula requirement:

```text
B = base price of the first step
S = increase in price per subsequent step
n = number of rank steps above the pricing baseline

TotalPrice(n) = B*n + S*n*(n-1)/2
UpgradePrice = TotalPrice(targetProgress) - TotalPrice(currentProgress)
```

Lower numeric ranks are better in the existing implementation; the default entry
rank is 101. The implemented baseline is the guild's current entry rank. With B=1 and S=0,
90 steps cost 90 tokens. With B=1 and S=0.1, they cost 490.5 tokens. B=S=0 allows
free progression within the purchase ceiling. Guild tokens have six decimal
places, so prices can use integer base units and checked wide intermediates.
The formula has a fixed number of operations and no loop over ranks.

The implementation charges only for steps climbed, including when buying back a
demoted rank; it does not retain a lifetime spending credit. Current and target
progress are calculated against the current entry rank at execution. A worse
numeric rank than entry has zero paid progress; a target must be at or better
than entry and strictly improve the current rank. Prices use integer token base
units, with no per-step rounding and a checked final uint64 amount. Member
purchases supply an expected current rank and maximum price; admission supplies
an expected current guild and maximum price. Stale expectations or a price above
the cap refuse the transaction before payment.

The guild's minimum purchasable rank defines the sale ceiling and may be 1.
Minimum 0 disables sales; purchase admission requires an enabled sale range.
Buying a rank supplies its configured operational grants. Promotions, demotions,
ownership transfer and permission administration require explicit administrative
authority from ownership or a direct grant; rank-derived `PermAdmin` is insufficient.
No numeric rank, including rank 1, confers administrative authority by itself.

### Separate purchase and admission transactions

Use two separate messages, with shared internal pricing and payment logic:

| Message | Preconditions | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `GuildRankPurchase` | Player already belongs to the target guild; target rank is purchasable | Debit player vault, credit guild vault, update rank atomically |
| `GuildMembershipJoinByRankPurchase` | Guild explicitly enables purchase admission; normal applicable membership-transition rules hold; target rank is purchasable | Debit player vault, credit guild vault, join at purchased rank atomically |

- Ordinary rank purchasing cannot bypass guild membership gates.
- Add a separate `joinByRankPurchaseEnabled` setting, disabled by default for
  existing and newly created guilds. This is an explicitly configured additional
  admission route, not an implicit consequence of publishing rank prices.
- When purchase admission is disabled, non-members must first join through the
  guild's existing acceptable routes before purchasing ranks.
- Keep existing direct joining, requests, invitations, base-rank assignment and
  configured infusion-related membership behavior. Players without guild tokens
  can still use these routes where the guild permits them.
- Preserve applicable leave/join restrictions, including the existing restriction
  on an owner leaving their current guild without transferring ownership first.
- A failed payment or membership transition must leave both rank and balances
  unchanged. Existing member purchase does not synthesize a new application.

### Guild capabilities and permissions

- Start with only the guild owner having the new guild capabilities by default.
- Map actions to logical existing permission bits where they fit. Use existing
  guild-rank object permission grants for guild-owned objects.
- Keep signing-key permission ceilings independent of player standing and asset
  ownership. A guild owner using a restricted secondary key cannot bypass them.
- Guild ownership is independent of membership. Explicit guild selection must
  work for an owner who is not a member of the guild they manage.
- Rank-derived authority follows current rank and membership: leaving or being
  kicked removes that authority immediately; demotion removes authority no longer
  supplied by the new rank. Resolve owner-derived authority against the current
  guild owner after an ownership transfer.
- Independent direct object grants remain until explicitly revoked, including
  grants previously made by an authorized administrator. Rank-derived operational
  authority cannot create new permanent grants.
  Departure, dismissal and demotion do not trigger new retrospective grant
  cleanup. Guild ownership transfer explicitly clears the outgoing owner's
  entire direct grant on that guild; independently granted child-object rights
  remain. Grant-provenance tracking and cascading revocation are out of scope. Existing grant/revoke checks and signing-key ceilings remain.
  Kick also preserves independent guild grants. Explicit administrators must
  revoke them separately; membership authority cannot erase administration.
- Benefits/authority redesign is out of scope. Security review must nevertheless
  require explicit administrative authority for promotions, demotions, permission
  grants and ownership transfers. A rank threshold for Admin cannot supply it.

### Guild creation

- Replace PoW and reactor-entitlement creation with one paid path.
- Cost: 2 whole Alpha. Burn 1 Alpha; deposit 1 Alpha into the new guild's token
  collateral pool; mint 1 whole guild token into the new guild vault.
- Require a primary reactor, but no ownership or control of that reactor. It can
  simply be the founder's preferred reactor. Direct joining counts an infusion already
  on that selected primary without redelegating it to the same validator or
  demanding token-migration authority. The infusion minimum still applies; other
  reactors associated with the guild remain eligible under the existing rules.
- Keep the primary-reactor concept. Existence and validator-health validation
  must be specified separately from the removed ownership requirement. The existing
  update handler requires a registered, non-jailed target validator and permits
  a healthy unbonded target. [Eleven signed application cases](v0.22.0-economy-security-review.md#signed-selection-of-a-different-primary-reactor)
  now verify different-reactor selection, independent control, key ceilings,
  explicit guild grants, typed events and multi-message rollback. The native GP1
  phase still covers a confirmed same-reactor update.
- Simplify obsolete charter CLI/proof/parameter paths while preserving historical
  stored data and wire compatibility through deliberate migration/deprecation.

### Reactor base power, diversity and stake concentration

The September 23 replacement supersedes the former five-band design. Eligible
reactor stake produces flat 1:1 base power before the existing commission split.
No ranking snapshots, grandfathered weights, entry-rate blends or cancellation
entitlements remain. Returning an unbonding to stake follows current backing and
cap checks; it does not restore a historical rate.

Aggregate each owner's commission-rounded delegator power by reactor, including
multiple owned addresses at the same reactor. For total power S and the sum of
squared per-reactor contributions Q, the effective reactor count is S²/Q. The
bonus scales with that count minus one, reaching a maximum of 5,000 basis points
at nine extra effective reactors. Calculate with exact integers and floor the
final bonus; clip applied bonus to the owner's remaining uint64 capacity room.
Generator output changes that room but is excluded from the diversity formula.
These economic parameters are configurable at genesis or through a reviewed
upgrade, not through an ordinary live parameter change.

A concentrated deposit can reduce total power, even after counting its added
base contribution. The user accepts this economic behavior. Apply the resulting
capacity decrease and ordinary GridCascade; clients should explain the effect.
Jailed/unbonded reactors lose live eligibility immediately, while bounded repair
brings stored infusions and bonuses into agreement. Queries distinguish stored
accounting from conservative usable power while that work is pending.

The default stake cap is 3,000 basis points and activates at ten bonded
validators. Activation is persisted and remains active if the validator count
later falls. Existing positions are not forcibly reduced to the cap, but native
and Structs inflows, redelegations and cancellations must pass it. Outflows,
slashing and validator departure remain possible. If a guild join requires a
stake migration that cannot complete, the whole join is rejected atomically.

Initialize cap state and rebuild diversity inside the complete v0.22.0 upgrade's
outer cache, after canonical backing/ownership and indexes are established.
Genesis reconstructs derived portfolios from staking-backed infusions instead
of re-adding an exported bonus. Typed cap and owner-diversity events, zero-bonus
updates and a first-block restart reset let consumers reconstruct current state.
See the [replacement plan](v0.22.0-reactor-replacement-plan.md) for arithmetic,
resource limits, hook boundaries and the complete verification matrix.

### Accepted upgrade consequences

The user accepts brownouts and the normal consequences of reduced energy
capacity. The economy is not yet real and can be changed to improve it. The user
subsequently approved replacing the rate bands with 1:1 base power for all
eligible stake plus a diversity bonus. Capacity repairs, normal health gates and
bonus reductions can still reduce total power and trigger ordinary cascade.
This acceptance does not waive conservation of assets, correct agreement
settlement, or chain liveness. Preserve existing contract behavior unless a
specific change is deliberately included in the final specification.

### Agreement/provider billing: preserve the existing model

Confirmed by the user on 2026-09-21:

- Calculate accrued earnings lazily from elapsed block counts and current
  agreement terms through the established provider checkpoints and settlement
  paths. This does not introduce a transfer to a wallet on every block.
- Preserve withdrawals, expiry, consumer/provider cancellation penalties,
  refunds and capacity/duration-change accounting. Vaults change where funds
  reside; guild ownership changes who may authorize an action.
- Let GridCascade destroy allocations and settle agreements as in the existing
  design. Do not introduce retrospective outage repricing or pause/resume billing.
- Preserve consumer collateral priority, exactly-once teardown, checkpoint-before-
  load-change ordering, send-enabled checks on voluntary payouts and all other
  applicable 117b/118b and current-worktree security fixes.
- Retain only the current state needed by that model, including agreement
  start/end heights, provider checkpoint height, loads, balances and indices.
  Continue emitting ordinary economic, transfer and deletion events for indexers.
- Remove the added historical journal/capture stores, historical supply proofs,
  retained claim/window audit records, separate service clock, historical
  financial snapshots/replay, related query surfaces and maintenance workers.
  Remove their activation/import/export hooks and obsolete test expectations.
- Preserve current-state backing/admission checks and bounded GridCascade;
  these protect live gameplay and are independent of retrospective billing.

Acceptance must compare exact provider earnings, consumer refunds, remaining
collateral and event transfers under healthy operation, expiry, each cancellation
path, GridCascade, term changes, rollback and both restart modes. Review the
existing lazy arithmetic and preserve security corrections; do not simply revert
whole files to 117b/118b or change expected balances to make tests pass.

## Implementation approach

### CurrentContext and entity caches

- Introduce a typed owner abstraction that resolves an existing player or guild,
  rather than passing guild ids through methods that assume a `PlayerCache`.
- Resolve vault addresses back to their owner through an index. Staking hooks
  must not create a synthetic player when they encounter a guild vault.
- Generalize the relevant ownership, infusion/grid contribution and permission
  interfaces together. Retain player-specific APIs for player-only gameplay.
- Use the existing per-operation cache system; register new caches in sorted
  `CommitAll` handling and extend architecture coverage.
- Explicitly manage SDK hook boundaries. A nested hook commit must not be
  overwritten by a previously loaded outer grid cache. Reconcile final staking
  state without duplicate ownership/capacity credit.
- Validate type, existence and liveness before authorization or mutation. Use
  scoped indices and update/delete each index under the same key.

### Public interfaces and clients

The served `docs/static/openapi.yml` is regenerated from the current message and
query schemas, including vault, reactor stake-share and owner-diversity interfaces. Existing paths
and operation IDs are preserved. `make proto-swagger` now invokes the checked-in
generator/merge helper against only the RPC service protos; the old all-proto
invocation failed on a dependency's module descriptor and did not update the
served document. Local schema references and the existing operation-ID set were
verified. Generated unbound message entries retain the existing documentation
convention; they do not replace signed transaction broadcasting.

- Migration events must fully inform indexers about newly created vault accounts,
  canonical ownership associations, ualpha and every other asset movement,
  staking/queue record relocation, and changed infusion/economic state. Review
  existing bank and association events before adding new ones; preserve their
  semantics. Verify exact payloads, deterministic order, rollback, reruns and
  delivery in the final upgrade block. Track evidence and remaining gaps in
  `v0.22.0-economy-events.md`.

- Add the two purchase messages, guild sale/admission configuration, vault queries
  and owner-aware asset/infrastructure operations.
- Preserve message creator as the authenticated signing identity; make the
  represented asset owner explicit where needed.
- Add purchase quote protection and observable payment/rank outcomes. The fields
  and CLI forms are defined in the [client reference](v0.22.0-economy-client-reference.md#rank-sales-and-admission).
  Preserve the documented distinction between structured Go errors and delivered
  ABCI codes rather than assuming they are interchangeable.
- Update proto sources, regenerate generated code, and update CLI/autocli,
  simulation operations, genesis, query pagination and event consumers' contracts.
- Review every applicable ante map and classifier for new and changed messages.
  Vault staking cannot become an unthrottled wrapper around native keeper calls.
- Events are a public API: add information deliberately and avoid silently
  repurposing player-specific fields to mean guild ownership.

## v0.22.0 migration and chain continuation

Migration objective: move all assets belonging to associated player addresses
into their player vaults. Leave addresses not associated with a player alone.
Investigate a complete migration before proposing exclusions; difficulty alone
is not a reason to leave assets behind.

The previous claim that public SDK APIs cannot rewrite staking queues/ID indices
was too broad. SDK v0.53.5 exposes `SetUBDQueueTimeSlice`,
`SetRedelegationQueueTimeSlice`, `SetUnbondingDelegationByUnbondingID` and
`SetRedelegationByUnbondingID`. Existing transaction helpers intentionally refuse
these transfers, but that does not prove a coordinated upgrade cannot do them.

Required investigation and implementation:

1. Inventory registered addresses and all affected bank balances, delegations,
   unbondings, redelegations, rewards, withdrawal addresses and vesting state.
   Include validator operator/self-delegation implications and non-Alpha assets.
2. Establish deterministic vault accounts and ownership/holder indices before
   transfers or hooks use them. Do not confuse collateral accounts with vaults.
3. Preserve the existing cascade-queue migration before any phase can enqueue.
   Preserve infusion fuel repair and self-defense cleanup; compose their ordering
   with vault migration and flat-power/diversity reconstruction rather than blindly appending work.
4. Move bank assets and assemble complete delegation transfers. Preserve total
   shares, reward entitlement, slashability, queue references, operation IDs,
   completion times and holds. Handle merges without erasing destination records.
5. Retain validator self-stake at the operator address under the approved
   exception, with real SDK proof of continued access and minimum-self-delegation
   enforcement. Classify pending records by the delegation they reference,
   including redelegation destination slashability. For a native SDK vesting
   account with retained self-stake or associated pending staking records, retain
   the entire account at its existing address: all balances, delegations, pending
   records, reward settings, vesting schedule and counters. Exclude that address
   consistently from all vault relocation phases. This deliberately also retains
   its non-self stake and liquid assets; other player-associated addresses still
   migrate. Vesting obligations stay with the native account under SDK rules even
   if the address's player association changes. Do not introduce schedule splitting,
   shared vesting counters, custom refund routing or obligation-transfer logic.
   Report the exception accurately without emitting asset-movement events for
   assets that stayed put. Verify retained-account access and normal address
   lifecycle protections with the real SDK.
6. Rebuild owner-aware infusion/grid state, flat backing and owner diversity,
   avoiding duplicate credits and preserving jailed-validator energy gating.
7. Validate bank/staking accounting, collateral obligations and state references.
   Let the bounded grid cascade process the accepted capacity loss.
8. Verify both height-preserving and zero-height export/restart. Preserve
   canonical backing, derived diversity, cap activation, replay nonces, queues,
   pending builds and clocks. Genesis reconstruction completes outstanding reactor
   repair; database reopening must retain and resume its pending work.

Whole-store scans are acceptable for an upgrade, but peak memory and execution
time still matter. Do not assume an existing helper's collection strategy scales
to migration cardinality. Do not rerun v0.21.0 repair phases indiscriminately;
some repairs are explicitly not idempotent.
