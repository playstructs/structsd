## Decisions resolved

- Rank authority follows current membership and rank; leaving or being kicked
  removes that authority. Independent direct grants, including self-directed
  grants made using rank authority, remain until explicitly revoked. No new
  cleanup on departure, dismissal, demotion or guild ownership transfer.
- Keep affected native vesting validator accounts intact at their addresses,
  including their other assets and stake. Vesting obligations remain with those
  SDK accounts; no custom obligation-transfer or shared-account machinery.

No further product decision is currently blocking implementation. The native
vesting exception is implemented with real-SDK and composed signed coverage;
combined migration and continuation verification also pass with the documented
fixture limits.

Raid vulnerability retains the existing explicit Command Ship transition rule;
no redesign is required for this release. Historical-checkpoint trust and outage
pause/resume decisions are closed by the confirmed ordinary-billing scope.

Audit infrastructure removal, the scoped security/resource review and final
native migration/lifecycle acceptance are complete. Chain-side event accuracy
and delivery are covered by that evidence. Downstream indexer work is separate;
no production release date or deployment is implied.
